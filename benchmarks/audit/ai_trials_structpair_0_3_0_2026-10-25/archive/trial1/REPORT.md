# Independent AI trial of TopoFold structpair 0.3.0

This trial completed installation, one KRAS smoke analysis, one independently specified Abl comparison, and real-browser viewer interaction. Both original results pass all recorded input, spec and output hash checks. The viewer rejected a copied result after a single numerical value was altered. No reproducible TopoFold defect was established in the tested paths. This is an independent **AI trial, not a human usability study**, and it is not an exhaustive correctness audit.

The shared Abl coordinates show concentrated local differences around author residues 398–419, with other differences near 249–255 and 295–300. These persist descriptively across the two selected 1OPJ copies. The comparison does not isolate a ligand, mutation, resistance mechanism or biological state. Construct, ligand, mutation and crystallographic conditions vary together.

The comparison answers the predeclared geometric question: the deposited shared kinase traces have concentrated local differences around author residues 398-419, with additional large differences near 249-255 and 295-300. This is an observation about these coordinates, not a causal account of ligand action or a functional-state assignment.

The primary-pair maximum curvature difference is 0.693221 rad at 410-412, also the maximum three-point span difference (1.4671 A) and local three-point RMSD (0.789025 A). At the corresponding four-point window 410-413, circular torsion difference is 3.0303 rad and local RMSD is 2.29761 A, both maxima. The four-point endpoint-distance difference instead peaks at 403-406 (4.55484 A). Evidence: windows.csv position 163 (CSV line 165), position 156 (line 158), and summary.json top entries; figure.png panels B-G.

The metrics tell related but different stories. At 249-252 (position 2, CSV line 4), torsion differs by 2.21874 rad and four-point RMSD by 1.71961 A, while the endpoint distance changes only 0.110435 A. An endpoint distance cannot retain handedness or fully specify four-point shape. Over all eligible primary windows the three-point rank correlations are 0.937 (angle versus span) and 0.981 (angle versus RMSD); torsion versus four-point span is 0.527. These are descriptive correlations between overlapping windows, not independent statistical tests or evidence of method superiority. Source: summary.json agreement, 280 three-point and 279 four-point windows.

The largest differences are not unique to the choice of 1OPJ chain A. At 410-413, both cross pairings give torsion differences 3.0089-3.0303 rad versus 0.021397 rad between J copies; four-point RMSD is 2.17589-2.29761 A across entries versus 0.167824 A within J. This increases confidence that this local observation persists for these two selected crystal copies. It does not estimate measurement uncertainty or establish significance. K has no within-entry comparison. Source: chain_variation.csv (position=163, channels d_tau and rmsd4), exact rows copied to interpretation-evidence.json.

Trimmed-fit displacement is a separate quantity: residue 409 has cross-pair range 32.8473-33.255 A, versus J-copy displacement 1.08438 A. It measures placement after a trimmed global fit, not four-residue shape and not a trajectory excursion. Global all-common-CA RMSD is 5.121181 A over 282 CA, with numpy Kabsch agreeing to 2.7e-14 A. Neither number can be substituted for local RMSD. Sources: chain_variation.csv position=161/channel=disp; summary.json global.

## Defects, gaps, environment failures and tester mistakes

**Reproducible TopoFold defects established:** none in the tested scope. Passing hashes show artifact integrity, not full mathematical correctness or universal usability. The included QCP/Kabsch agreement is a limited internal cross-check, not an independent validation of every local metric.

**Documentation/usability gaps or potential confusion:** the smoke downloader has no destination option, requiring relocation to obey this trial's separate-directory constraint; exclusion summary counts cause rows rather than unique windows; A/B denote both entries and author chain IDs; position indexes and hinge residues differ from window starts; trimmed-fit displacement and local RMSD use different fits. Wide tables and explanatory text were clipped at the right edge in the current browser viewport. The displayed warnings and provenance helped interpretation, but navigating wide scientific tables through accessibility/coordinates was cumbersome. No blanket claim about a human user's experience follows.

**Environment failures:** default sandbox curl could not resolve github.com (exit 6); authorized networking worked. Browser screenshot writes failed with EROFS (including /tmp), and a direct screenshot-byte fetch failed; a local trial form saved the bytes successfully. The user-confirmed usage-limit interruption stopped evidence capture and later required a viewer restart. None required a TopoFold source patch.

**Tester mistakes:** reading AGENTS before clone completion; wrong semantic role for segmented metric controls; attempting to click a canvas accessibility row without a layout object; cell-focus versus row-selection misclicks; coordinate/viewport mismatch selecting 162 instead of intended 163; reused browser variable absent after interruption; search/header attempts without a visible sort change. The corruption script first requested nonexistent column d_tau (actual d_tau_rad), then attempted to hash a work directory as a file. The first error changed nothing; the second happened after the one-cell copy edit. Recovery verified exactly one changed cell and every other copied file unchanged, without repeating the edit. See tamper-test.json. These failures are not hidden or attributed to TopoFold.

All edits stayed within the separate trial area. Final clone status is only `!! Cargo.lock`, generated and ignored by the build. No tracked source file changed; no commit, push or shipped-result overwrite occurred. This is verified in final-verification.log.
