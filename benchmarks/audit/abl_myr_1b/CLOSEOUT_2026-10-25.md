# ABL-MYR-1b closeout (2026-10-25)

**ABL-MYR-1b is closed. Its result is final for this protocol.** The comparison was run once
(results/ABL-MYR-1b_results.json). It was not rerun, no threshold was changed, and no frozen specification, measurement or
result was edited for this closeout. The closeout adds only a provenance inventory, generated by
[`closeout_inventory.py`](closeout_inventory.py), and this note.

ABL-MYR-1b is separate from the Ayaz WT/M472I route and from the HDX request. It is not evidence about either.

## Result as recorded (negative for TopoFold-specific signal)

These values are read from `results/ABL-MYR-1b_results.json` and `results/ABL-MYR-1b_residue_summary.json`.

- All gates G0 to G6 passed.
- The frozen decision rule reached step 4, with the outcome label `baselines cover TopoFold (post-hoc, descriptive)`.
- TopoFold flags no residue outside the ±2-residue neighbourhood of the residues flagged by the equal-information Cα
  baselines: `R_T_outside_N2_R_B` is empty.
  - The baselines are d(i,i+2), d(i,i+3) and RMSF.
- The κ/τ windows flagged in both sets (`R_T`) are covered by baseline windows.
- The φ/ψ comparator receives stronger information. It is reported separately and does not enter the decision.

## Limits (unchanged from the protocol and the run)

- **Post-hoc.** The protocol was designed after the start files and run inputs had been read.
- **Review.** It was reviewed by an independent AI review, not by human peer review.
- **Descriptive only.** There is no p-value and no inference (D33). The leave-one-run-out and cross-set filters are
  descriptive.
- **Source.**
  - One published simulation study, one force field.
  - One modelled kinase domain: residues 254–504, backbone N, CA and C, 251 residues.
  - The two thermostat sets are a consistency check, not independent replication.
- **αI.** Residues 518–531 are not in the topology, so no αI or Myr-pocket statement is made.
- **Sampling.** Frames are 2 ns apart, which supports a geometry description, not kinetics.
- **Comparability.** The criteria K1–K5 passed, which does not rule out confounds that were not examined.
- **Not shown.** This result does not show that TopoFold lacks value elsewhere. It shows that, for this dataset and this
  frozen rule, the κ/τ signal adds no residue beyond what the Cα baselines flag.

## Recovery

The step-by-step recovery and the exact commands are in the inventory (`recovery_steps`, `commands`). In short:
1. Check out this branch.
2. Rebuild the two environments.
3. Download the three archives from Dryad in a browser.
4. Verify them with `sha256sum -c results/ABL-MYR-1b_closeout_SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Inspecting the recorded result needs no rerun. A regeneration for method development should run the comparison into
new `--out` and `--work` directories, so the recorded files are not overwritten. That run is a new run and is reported as
one; it does not replace this result.
