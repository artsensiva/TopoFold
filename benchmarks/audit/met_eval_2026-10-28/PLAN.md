The owner authorized, on 2026-10-28, a limited internal evaluation using the pinned public assay tables on the documented article (CC BY) and repository (MIT licence file) basis. The authorization does not establish commercial or redistribution rights; the source assay tables are not published here; nobody is contacted.

**Target population:** variants passing the measured no-drug eligibility rule (missense, numeric effects in both conditions, DMSO ROSACE.LOF == FALSE). The evaluation does not demonstrate prediction for variants whose baseline function is unknown.

## Folds, models, metric
- Outer: 10 contiguous blocks of positions (sizes 29 x 7 and 28 x 3), identical for all feature sets, inhibitors and models; buffer 8 residues; inner: 5 contiguous blocks of the sorted training positions with the same buffer, ridge alpha only; grouping: all substitutions and all inhibitors at a position are in the same fold. Cells: a held-out block with no eligible row for an inhibitor is recorded as empty_test and contributes nothing; fewer than 50 training rows -> every feature set and model of that cell uses the training mean (flagged, identical for all sets); zero training rows -> the cell is flagged no_training_rows and the evaluation is reported as a gate failure with partial results; no inhibitor or fold is dropped silently. Inner folds with an empty validation set or fewer than 20 training rows are skipped. Limits: blocking is in sequence only; residues close in space but far in sequence remain correlated; shared controls and libraries make inhibitors correlated.
- Models: ridge: per-inhibitor ridge; standardization from training rows only (inside every outer and inner fit); alpha in [0.1, 1.0, 10.0, 100.0, 1000.0]; GBM: per-inhibitor HistGradientBoostingRegressor({'max_depth': 3, 'learning_rate': 0.05, 'max_iter': 200, 'min_samples_leaf': 20, 'l2_regularization': 1.0, 'random_state': 0, 'early_stopping': False}); no validation split of any kind.
- Primary metric: position-weighted mean squared error of gamma: per inhibitor, squared errors averaged per position, then over positions; then equal-weight mean over the six inhibitors; Delta = MSE(a) - MSE(b), negative favours a. Secondary: relative MSE reduction (MSE(b)-MSE(a))/MSE(b); per-block and per-inhibitor Delta; per-inhibitor Pearson and Spearman correlation of out-of-fold predictions with gamma (pooled rows). Sensitivity: same out-of-fold predictions re-scored on rows whose approximate sd_gamma is at or below the median (no refit).

## Comparisons and decisions
- C4 (F3 vs F2): does the angular encoding beat the Cα distances computed from the same nine coordinates?  C3 (F3 vs F1): does it add beyond ordinary site context? All six comparisons are reported; none is selected afterwards. Comparisons: C1 = F1 vs F0, C2 = F2 vs F1, C3 = F3 vs F1, C4 = F3 vs F2, C5 = F5 vs F4, C6 = F4 vs F1.
- Criterion met: Delta < 0 AND the upper end of the 95% moving-block-bootstrap percentile interval < 0 AND Delta < 0 in at least 8 of 10 outer blocks AND in at least 5 of 6 inhibitors. Not met: Delta >= 0, or Delta < 0 in at most 5 of 10 blocks. Otherwise inconclusive. an inconclusive interval or a small point difference is never read as equivalence. Bootstrap: blocks of 20 consecutive positions, all inhibitors and models resampled together, 2000 resamples, seed 20261028; descriptive, conditional on this dataset and procedure, coverage not assumed.
- Families: criterion met in both families; criterion met in one family only: family-dependent, not supported; criterion not met in either family (not met or inconclusive).
- Engineering outcome (5% relative MSE reduction (provisional engineering continuation threshold; not demonstrated practical or commercial usefulness)): continue (angular): C3 criterion met in both families with relative reduction >= 5% in both, and C4 criterion met in both families; continue (geometry): C3 as above but C4 not met in both families; otherwise: otherwise (not equivalence, not proof of no effect).
- Uncertainty context: approximate sd_gamma = sqrt(sd_inh^2 + sd_DMSO^2) assumes zero covariance between estimates that share controls; the covariance is unknown, so this is context and a row filter for one sensitivity analysis, not a measurement-error model and not a detectable-improvement bound.
- External baseline: The authors report correlations between predicted and observed fitness scores. Our endpoint is gamma (inhibitor minus no-drug score) on no-drug-eligible variants, evaluated by position blocks with a buffer, per inhibitor, from wild-type-template features; theirs is a pooled model trained with predicted mutant-complex structure features and tested on held-out inhibitors, 20% of random positions and two amino acids. The endpoints and splits differ, so no numerical comparison or superiority ranking is made; the ESM arm (F4, F5) is the only external-model comparison, and their model was not recreated.
