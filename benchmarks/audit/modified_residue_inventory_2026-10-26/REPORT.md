## Recommendation

**A narrowly scoped modified-residue support task is warranted.** Decision rule, fixed in the script before the result was read: narrow support task if at least one observed modified residue with backbone is excluded by the adapter and at least one window touches a gap that contains it; no problem if no modified residue is lost; otherwise insufficient evidence.
Evidence that the adapter drops a name is also in the diagnostic tests (`tests/test_modified_residue_inventory.py`: SEP, TPO and PTR are absent from the real adapter output; MSE and standard residues are read).

If the task is taken (not implemented here), acceptance tests:
1. SYNTHETIC chains with SEP, TPO, PTR (HETATM records) and a standard parent: the residue is read as a position with its Cα; its **deposited name and MODRES parent/annotation are kept** in residues.csv and the manifest, never replaced by the parent name.
2. Chain continuity: C(i-1)-N(i) bonding, numbering adjacency and the 4.2 Å Cα–Cα rule are applied exactly as for standard residues; a modified residue does not bridge a real gap.
3. The two PIM1 pairs gain exactly their previously excluded windows (counts from Table 3) and nothing else; all other pairs' outputs are byte-identical; windows around other gaps stay excluded with their causes.
4. A modified residue without Cα or without peptide bonds stays excluded with a named cause; an unannotated non-standard residue with backbone atoms is reported, not silently read or dropped.
5. Reference checks: κ/τ and local RMSD at the newly included windows against the independent references of KIN-PAIR-1; invariance under rigid motion; the residue-name mismatch policy (`flag`) marks windows with a modified residue.
6. Historical schemas and outputs remain readable and unchanged unless the new behaviour is requested.
