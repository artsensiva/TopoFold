# Calibration Phase Closeout (2026-10-23)

**Scope:** closes the inferential-calibration phase (REPCHAR-1, PERM-1, NULLSTRESS-1, DABOOT-1). Frozen
specifications, code and results are unchanged. No further bootstrap or permutation variant is started now.

## Qualifications on DABOOT-1

- The failure is measured for one configuration: 5 replicas × 387 frames per condition; AR(1) correlation 0, 0.8,
  0.95; stationary SYNTHETIC generators; automatic Politis–White block length; Kuiper statistic; B = 499;
  α = 0.05.
- It does not show that block bootstraps are invalid in general. With longer runs or more replicas the same method
  may behave differently; that is untested here.
- 200 repetitions per cell give coarse Monte Carlo precision. The failure verdict rests on the predeclared criterion,
  which every null cell met (rejection > 0.10, or Wilson lower bound > 0.05). It does not rest on a precise size
  estimate.
- The positive controls (1.000) cannot be read as useful sensitivity while the null is miscalibrated.
- The automatic block lengths at φ = 0.95 (median about 33 frames, against a theoretical optimum of about 53) are
  descriptive. They are a plausible contributor, not a demonstrated cause, and nothing was retuned.
- Real MD was never scored inferentially in this phase. DEV-ENS-1 and DEV-REP-1 remain descriptive development
  evidence.

## Conditions for reopening inference

All must hold, and the decision must be recorded in the decision log:
1. **A defined biological comparison** is selected, with accessible, permitted data (for example ABL WT vs M472I),
   and it justifies the statistical investment.
2. **The data are first described with the descriptive comparison tool** (`python -m topofold_inputs.ensemble`):
   replicas per condition, run length, and the temporal diagnostics per condition.
3. **The data carry substantially more independent information than 5 × 387 frames at φ ≈ 0.95.** That means runs
   much longer than the measured decorrelation lag, or materially more replicas per condition, preferably from
   independent starting states. The amount is set from the measured diagnostics, not assumed.
4. **Any candidate method is specified and calibrated in advance** on SYNTHETIC data matched to those dimensions and
   measured correlations, under frozen null and sensitivity criteria, before any real-data scoring.
5. **Interpretation stays bounded:** a calibrated test of marginal distributions still does not establish
   equilibrium, mechanism or biological function.

Until then, ensemble comparisons are descriptive: no p-values, significance labels or unvalidated uncertainty
intervals.
