## 5. Limitations

- AdK is a development tier on two trajectories of one protein, chosen and inspected in WP-B; results are descriptive and cannot support claims about other proteins.
- Query and database frames come from the same trajectory separated by a gap; they are not independent and the runs drift (WP-B), so query and database frames may sample different regimes (protocol §5).
- Medians and IQRs are across windows, which overlap and are correlated; no interval or test is reported.
- The φ/ψ target is a reference description of local backbone conformation, not ground truth for function. Cα-only inputs do not contain peptide-plane orientation; how much of the φ/ψ variation they can recover is what is measured here, not assumed.
- xtc coordinates are quantized to 0.01 Å; all methods receive identical values (A2).
- B3 uses the production κ (turning angle, π minus the virtual bond angle); the RMS difference is identical for either convention.

## 6. Interpretation notes (editorial closeout, added after execution; results unchanged)

These notes were added after the run, without rerunning; the results and provenance files are unchanged (see `results/wpd_adk_editorial_log.json`).

**C1 is compared on a different, conditional coverage.** C1 has no result for queries whose window mean is ill-conditioned (R < 0.1), and database frames with R < 0.1 are ranked last as +∞ (A1). Its aggregates therefore average over a subset of queries and rank a reduced effective candidate set, while M1, M2, B1, B2 and B3 use all valid queries and candidates. The C1 rows describe performance *conditional on a well-conditioned mean*; they are not a like-for-like comparison with the other methods. The random reference uses all database frames. **The cis-peptide count does not establish the dihedral sign convention.** The criterion |ω| < π/2 is invariant to the sign of ω, so the single cis bond observed (Phe86–Pro87, matching the CISPEP records of 4AKE and 1AKE) is consistent with the backbone atom mapping but says nothing about the sign of φ, ψ or τ. The sign convention rests on the WP-A IUPAC reference checks and on `test_dihedral_known_values` in `test_wpd.py`. An earlier chat summary of this run stated otherwise; that statement was wrong and is withdrawn.

**Competing methods.** M1/M2 (ordered τ), B1 (Cα–Cα distances), B2 (window Cα RMSD) and B3 (κ) remain competing candidates for the held-out tier. The AdK orderings above are descriptive, from one protein, and do not pre-select a winner.
