# SYNTHETIC_PERM-1: Prospective Evaluation of a Replica-Level Permutation Method — Specification (2026-10-23)

**Status:** frozen with code by `benchmarks/audit/synth_rep_perm/run_perm.py freeze` before the deciding run. The
machine-readable grid is `benchmarks/audit/synth_rep_perm/run_spec.json`.

**Scope:** one prospective method, evaluated on the existing SYNTHETIC generators and scenarios only. No real data,
no real feature arrays, no rescoring of historical experiments. DEV-ENS-1, DEV-REP-1 and REPCHAR-1 artifacts are
preserved.

## Cells and draws

- **Cells:** the 81 five-versus-five REPCHAR-1 cells (S0, S5, S1, S2, S3, S4a, S4b, S4c), with their original cell
  identifiers and seeds. The three S6 replica-count cells are deferred.
- **Draws:** generated by the frozen REPCHAR-1 `simulate()` and `synthetic_generators.py`, imported unchanged and
  hash-recorded.
- **Repetitions:** 200 per cell. Both methods are evaluated on the **same** histograms in every repetition.
- **Run-time check:** the historical screen's category counts on these draws must reproduce the REPCHAR-1 results
  cell by cell (recorded).

## Methods

- **Historical screen:** `topofold_inputs.compare.replica_comparison`, with its complete-separation categories
  (consistent, replica-dependent, inconclusive).
- **Prospective method:** `topofold_inputs.replica_permutation.permutation_test`.
  - Unit: one replica histogram. JS distances (base 2) are precomputed between all ten replicas.
  - Statistic: T = mean between-group distance − ½ (within-A mean + within-B mean).
  - Enumeration: all 252 labelled allocations into two groups of five, the observed allocation included.
  - Probability: exact upper tail, p = #{T_perm ≥ T_obs − tol} / 252. **Tie rule:** tol = 10⁻¹² · max(1, |T_obs|);
    ties count toward p.
  - Decision: reject at **α = 0.05** (fixed). Because each partition appears twice, p ≤ 0.05 means at most 12 of the
    252 allocations, a nominal size of 12/252 ≈ 0.048 under exact exchangeability without ties.

## Exchangeability and scenario roles

Under the null, the joint distribution of the ten replica histograms must be invariant to permuting replica labels
across conditions. Roles are assigned from the generating assumptions before the run:

| Role | Cells | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Primary identical-distribution null | S0 | Rejection frequency is compared with the nominal 0.048 |
| Heterogeneous exchangeable null | S4b, Δ = 0 | Replica offsets are iid across both conditions, so exchangeability holds |
| Single-replica perturbation (not an ordinary null) | S4a Δ = 0, S4c | One unusual replica in condition A only: the condition samples differ by construction, and exchangeability is violated |
| Condition-confounded sampling (not a null) | S5 | Realized distributions differ through a shared within-condition offset; a rejection cannot resolve the causal confounding |
| Condition effect | S1, S2, S3, S4a Δ = 1, S4b Δ = 1 | Sensitivity |

## Reporting

- Per cell, each with Wilson 95 % intervals (200 repetitions give coarse Monte Carlo precision):
  - the screen's consistent, replica-dependent and inconclusive frequencies, reported separately;
  - the permutation rejection frequency.
- Per cell, 2 × 2 agreement counts (permutation reject vs screen call), with screen calls kept split into consistent
  and replica-dependent.
- Null behaviour and sensitivity are assessed together; a higher positive rate alone is not an improvement.
- These are per-evaluation SYNTHETIC results, not a multiple-window significance procedure.
- Nothing (statistic, α, tie rule, generators, bins, reporting) is changed after outcomes are seen.

## Budget and partial results

- **Cap:** 900 CPU-s, enforced by a hard `RLIMIT_CPU`.
- **Stopping rule:** before each cell, the runner stops if the CPU used + 1.5 × the mean CPU per repetition × 200
  would exceed the cap.
- **Cells:** all-or-nothing.
- **Checkpoints:** the results file is rewritten after each cell with status `partial`. It becomes `complete` only
  when all 81 planned cells have finished.
- **Environment:** the Python, NumPy and platform versions are recorded with the freeze.
