We are interested in the HDX-MS measurements of Abl kinase reported in Ayaz et al., "Structural mechanism of a
drug-binding process involving a large conformational change of the protein target", *Nature Communications*
14:1885 (2023), doi:10.1038/s41467-023-36956-5. The raw data are deposited as PRIDE PXD034008.

**About us.** TopoFold is a research prototype for describing conformational changes in molecular ensembles. It is
not a commercial product, but it has future commercial ambitions, and we want our use of data to be clearly within
your terms.

**Intended use.** A method evaluation comparing the backbone-geometry differences we compute from MD ensembles of
apo wild-type and M472I Abl with the peptide-level deuterium-uptake differences you measured. Any comparison would be
reported with its limitations, including negative results.

**Request.** In the PRIDE record and the paper's Source Data we found the raw files and the peptide list, but not
processed uptake values. Would you be willing to share:
1. processed peptide-level deuterium uptake (e.g. the DynamX state or cluster export) for **apo WT and apo M472I** at
   each labelling time, with **replicate values** (or means with spreads) and the peptide map in the same numbering;
2. the residue numbering of the construct relative to human c-Abl, and whether any samples contained imatinib or
   other ligands;
3. any back-exchange or maximally-deuterated control information that should accompany relative uptake values.

**Permissions.** We would be grateful for a separate answer to each:
1. use of the processed data for internal method evaluation;
2. publication of derived results (summary comparisons and figures), with citation of your paper and the PRIDE
   dataset;
3. redistribution of the processed values, or whether they must stay private;
4. whether later commercial use would require a separate agreement.
